TWISTPRSAT THEOREM CHALLENGES

Source paper:
  V. Borja Macias, M. E. Coniglio, and A. Hernandez-Tello,
  "Probabilities beyond Belnap-Dunn logic: dealing with gaps, gluts and
  reliability," arXiv:2608.20228v1.

HOW TO USE THIS PACK

Each numbered file below is separately loadable in TwistPrSAT.  Each file
asserts that a theorem from the paper fails.  Select SMT (Z3), load one file
from the `theorems` folder, and press Find Model.  The expected result is
UNSAT.  An UNSAT result means that there is no LET_K+ probability model that
is a counterexample to the encoded theorem.

Notation used below:
  ~A       is the paper's De Morgan negation, not A.
  oA       is the reliability/classicality formula, circle A.
  cnot A   is the paper's classical negation, sim A, defined as A -> false.
  Pr(A)    is the paper's one-dimensional probability p(A).

For the region-decomposition results, the six regions induced by A are:
  B°_A = A & oA                         reliable belief
  B_A  = A & cnot (~A v oA)             belief
  C_A  = A & ~A                         conflict
  D_A  = ~A & cnot (A v oA)             disbelief
  D°_A = ~A & oA                        reliable disbelief
  U_A  = cnot (A v ~A)                  uncertainty

WHICH RESULTS ARE DISTINCTIVE OF LET_K+?

The most theory-specific universal results in this pack are 01, 02, and
05-08, together with the gap-glut balance identity in 11.  They essentially
involve the De Morgan negation ~, the reliability operator o, or the privileged
six-region partition.  Examples 12-14 are SAT countermodels demonstrating
three ways in which LET_K+ departs from classical probability: conflict can
have positive probability, excluded middle can have probability below 1, and
the De Morgan negation need not receive complementary probability.

Examples 03, 04, 09, and 10 are useful checks but are not distinctive.  They
are classical complement, inclusion-exclusion, entailment, and difference
laws recovered inside the richer language by using cnot where a Boolean
complement is required.

The paper's soundness/completeness results (Corollaries 6-8) and the semantic-
syntactic equivalence for six-valued Jeffrey updating (Proposition 15) are
also theory-specific meta-theorems.  They are not encoded here as single input
files: the former quantify over entire classes of probability functions and
models, while the current TwistPrSAT application does not implement Jeffrey
updates.


01_reliable_contradiction_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Proposition 4(3).  For every formula phi and every LET_K+ probability
    function p,

      p(phi & ~phi & ophi) = 0.

    The formula phi & ~phi & ophi is a bottom particle: reliability excludes
    a state that both supports and rejects phi.

  Instance tested:
      p(A & ~A & oA) = 0.

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A & ~A & oA) > 0

  Expected result: UNSAT.


02_reliability_bound_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Proposition 4(4).  For every formula phi,

      p(ophi) <= p(phi v ~phi).

    This follows from the LET_K+ inference ophi entails phi v ~phi and the
    monotonicity of p.

  Instance tested:
      p(oA) <= p(A v ~A).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(oA) > Pr(A v ~A)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


03_classical_negation_complement_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Proposition 4(7).  If cnot phi is the classical negation of phi, then

      p(cnot phi) = 1 - p(phi).

    Unlike the De Morgan negation ~, cnot forms a genuine Boolean complement.

  Instance tested:
      p(cnot A) = 1 - p(A).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(cnot A) != 1 - Pr(A)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


04_inclusion_exclusion_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Definition 10, Ax1.4.  For all formulas phi and psi,

      p(phi) + p(psi) = p(phi & psi) + p(phi v psi).

    This is the inclusion-exclusion axiom for the positive extensions of the
    two formulas.

  Instance tested:
      p(A) + p(B) = p(A & B) + p(A v B).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A) + Pr(B) != Pr(A & B) + Pr(A v B)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


05_positive_region_decomposition_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Corollary 3, the translation from P1 to P6.  The positive probability of
    phi is the sum of reliable-belief, belief, and conflict mass:

      p(phi) = p(phi_B°) + p(phi_B) + p(phi_C).

  Instance tested using the region formulas displayed above:
      p(A) = p(A & oA)
             + p(A & cnot (~A v oA))
             + p(A & ~A).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A) != Pr(A & oA) + Pr(A & cnot (~A v oA)) + Pr(A & ~A)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


06_negative_region_decomposition_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Corollary 3, the translation from P1 to P6.  The negative probability of
    phi, namely p(~phi), is the sum of reliable-disbelief, disbelief, and
    conflict mass:

      p(~phi) = p(phi_D°) + p(phi_D) + p(phi_C).

  Instance tested:
      p(~A) = p(~A & oA)
              + p(~A & cnot (A v oA))
              + p(A & ~A).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(~A) != Pr(~A & oA) + Pr(~A & cnot (A v oA)) + Pr(A & ~A)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


07_reliability_region_decomposition_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Corollary 2, the translation between P3 and P6.  The reliability
    coordinate is the sum of reliable-belief and reliable-disbelief mass:

      p(ophi) = p(phi_B°) + p(phi_D°).

  Instance tested:
      p(oA) = p(A & oA) + p(~A & oA).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(oA) != Pr(A & oA) + Pr(~A & oA)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


08_six_region_partition_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Proposition 2 and Definition 12, Ax6.3.  The six regions B°, B, C, D, D°,
    and U form a partition, so their probabilities sum to 1:

      p(phi_B°) + p(phi_B) + p(phi_C)
        + p(phi_D) + p(phi_D°) + p(phi_U) = 1.

  Instance tested:
      p(A & oA)
        + p(A & cnot (~A v oA))
        + p(A & ~A)
        + p(~A & cnot (A v oA))
        + p(~A & oA)
        + p(cnot (A v ~A)) = 1.

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      The same six-term sum != 1.

  Expected result: UNSAT.


09_entailment_identity_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Proposition 4(8).  For all formulas phi and psi, if psi entails phi, then

      p(phi & psi) = p(psi).

  Instance tested:
    Let psi be A & B and let phi be A.  Since A & B entails A, the theorem
    gives

      p(A & (A & B)) = p(A & B).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A & (A & B)) != Pr(A & B)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


10_classical_difference_identity_unsat.txt

  Paper result:
    Proposition 4(9).  For all formulas phi and psi,

      p(phi & cnot psi) = p(phi) - p(phi & psi).

    This is the ordinary difference identity for the classical complement of
    psi; it does not replace cnot psi with the De Morgan negation ~psi.

  Instance tested:
      p(A & cnot B) = p(A) - p(A & B).

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A & cnot B) != Pr(A) - Pr(A & B)

  Expected result: UNSAT.


11_gap_glut_balance_unsat.txt

  Distinctive derived result:
    Let c_A = p(A & ~A) be conflict mass and let
    u_A = p(cnot (A v ~A)) = 1 - p(A v ~A) be uncertainty mass.  Then

      p(A) + p(~A) = 1 + c_A - u_A.

    Equivalently,

      p(A) + p(~A)
        = 1 + p(A & ~A) - p(cnot (A v ~A)).

    This follows from inclusion-exclusion together with the definitions of
    conflict and uncertainty.  Classical probability is the limiting case
    c_A = u_A = 0, in which the right side is 1.

  Negated challenge loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A) + Pr(~A) != 1 + Pr(A & ~A) - Pr(cnot (A v ~A))

  Expected result: UNSAT.


12_conflict_possible_sat.txt

  Distinctive nonclassical possibility:
    LET_K+ permits positive probability for a glut:

      p(A & ~A) > 0.

    This differs from classical probability, where a proposition conjoined
    with its Boolean negation is impossible.  Here ~ is De Morgan negation,
    so A & ~A identifies the conflict region C_A.

  Model request loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A & ~A) > 0

  Expected result: SAT.


13_uncertainty_possible_sat.txt

  Distinctive nonclassical possibility:
    LET_K+ permits a gap, so excluded middle for De Morgan negation can have
    probability below 1:

      p(A v ~A) < 1.

    The missing mass is the uncertainty region
    u_A = p(cnot (A v ~A)).

  Model request loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(A v ~A) < 1

  Expected result: SAT.


14_demorgan_noncomplementarity_sat.txt

  Distinctive nonclassical possibility:
    The De Morgan negation ~A need not have the complementary probability of
    A:

      p(~A) != 1 - p(A).

    Proposition 4(7) gives the complement law only for the classical negation
    cnot A.  Conflict and uncertainty allow p(~A) to depart from 1-p(A).

  Model request loaded into TwistPrSAT:
      Pr(~A) != 1 - Pr(A)

  Expected result: SAT.
