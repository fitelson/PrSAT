TWISTPRSAT: ALL EXAMPLES

Source paper:
  V. Borja Macias, M. E. Coniglio, and A. Hernandez-Tello,
  "Probabilities beyond Belnap-Dunn logic: dealing with gaps, gluts and
  reliability," arXiv:2608.20228v1.

HOW TO USE THIS FILE

This master file contains both SAT and UNSAT examples.  Do not load the whole
file at once.  Copy the constraint lines from one numbered block into
TwistPrSAT, or load the corresponding ready-to-load file named in that block.

Notation:
  ~A       De Morgan negation
  oA       reliability/classicality
  cnot A   classical negation, defined as A -> false
  Pr(A)    positive probability of A

Select SMT (Z3) for every UNSAT challenge.  Random Search can certify SAT by
returning an exact rational witness, but an unsuccessful random search means
unknown rather than UNSAT.


EXAMPLE 00: UNIFORM SIX-REGION MODEL
File: TwistPrSAT_examples.txt
Expected result: SAT in Z3 and Random Search.

This model assigns probability 1/6 to each of the six regions B°, B, C, D, D°,
and U.  The remaining lines check the derived positive, negative, reliability,
excluded-middle, classical-complement, and conditional probabilities.

Pr(A & oA) = 1/6
Pr(A & cnot (~A v oA)) = 1/6
Pr(A & ~A) = 1/6
Pr(~A & cnot (A v oA)) = 1/6
Pr(~A & oA) = 1/6
Pr(cnot (A v ~A)) = 1/6
Pr(A) = 1/2
Pr(~A) = 1/2
Pr(oA) = 1/3
Pr(A v ~A) = 5/6
Pr(cnot A) = 1/2
Pr(oA | A) = 1/3
Pr(~A | A) = 1/3


EXAMPLE 01: RELIABLE CONTRADICTION IS IMPOSSIBLE
File: theorems/01_reliable_contradiction_unsat.txt
Paper result: Proposition 4(3), p(phi & ~phi & ophi) = 0.
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(A & ~A & oA) > 0


EXAMPLE 02: RELIABILITY EXCLUDES A GAP
File: theorems/02_reliability_bound_unsat.txt
Paper result: Proposition 4(4), p(ophi) <= p(phi v ~phi).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(oA) > Pr(A v ~A)


EXAMPLE 03: CLASSICAL NEGATION IS A COMPLEMENT
File: theorems/03_classical_negation_complement_unsat.txt
Paper result: Proposition 4(7), p(cnot phi) = 1 - p(phi).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(cnot A) != 1 - Pr(A)


EXAMPLE 04: INCLUSION-EXCLUSION
File: theorems/04_inclusion_exclusion_unsat.txt
Paper result: Definition 10, Ax1.4,
  p(phi)+p(psi) = p(phi & psi)+p(phi v psi).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(A) + Pr(B) != Pr(A & B) + Pr(A v B)


EXAMPLE 05: POSITIVE REGION DECOMPOSITION
File: theorems/05_positive_region_decomposition_unsat.txt
Paper result: Corollary 3,
  p(phi) = p(phi_B°)+p(phi_B)+p(phi_C).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(A) != Pr(A & oA) + Pr(A & cnot (~A v oA)) + Pr(A & ~A)


EXAMPLE 06: NEGATIVE REGION DECOMPOSITION
File: theorems/06_negative_region_decomposition_unsat.txt
Paper result: Corollary 3,
  p(~phi) = p(phi_D°)+p(phi_D)+p(phi_C).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(~A) != Pr(~A & oA) + Pr(~A & cnot (A v oA)) + Pr(A & ~A)


EXAMPLE 07: RELIABILITY REGION DECOMPOSITION
File: theorems/07_reliability_region_decomposition_unsat.txt
Paper result: Corollary 2,
  p(ophi) = p(phi_B°)+p(phi_D°).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(oA) != Pr(A & oA) + Pr(~A & oA)


EXAMPLE 08: SIX-REGION PARTITION
File: theorems/08_six_region_partition_unsat.txt
Paper result: Proposition 2 and Definition 12, Ax6.3.  The six region
probabilities sum to 1.
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(A & oA) + Pr(A & cnot (~A v oA)) + Pr(A & ~A) + Pr(~A & cnot (A v oA)) + Pr(~A & oA) + Pr(cnot (A v ~A)) != 1


EXAMPLE 09: ENTAILMENT IDENTITY
File: theorems/09_entailment_identity_unsat.txt
Paper result: Proposition 4(8).  If psi entails phi, then
  p(phi & psi)=p(psi).
Instance: psi=A & B and phi=A.
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(A & (A & B)) != Pr(A & B)


EXAMPLE 10: CLASSICAL DIFFERENCE IDENTITY
File: theorems/10_classical_difference_identity_unsat.txt
Paper result: Proposition 4(9),
  p(phi & cnot psi)=p(phi)-p(phi & psi).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(A & cnot B) != Pr(A) - Pr(A & B)


EXAMPLE 11: GAP-GLUT BALANCE IDENTITY
File: theorems/11_gap_glut_balance_unsat.txt
Distinctive derived result:
  p(A)+p(~A) = 1+p(A & ~A)-p(cnot (A v ~A)).
Equivalently, p(A)+p(~A)=1+conflict(A)-uncertainty(A).
Expected result: UNSAT.

Pr(A) + Pr(~A) != 1 + Pr(A & ~A) - Pr(cnot (A v ~A))


EXAMPLE 12: POSITIVE CONFLICT IS POSSIBLE
File: theorems/12_conflict_possible_sat.txt
Distinctive nonclassical possibility: p(A & ~A)>0.
Expected result: SAT.

Pr(A & ~A) > 0


EXAMPLE 13: POSITIVE UNCERTAINTY IS POSSIBLE
File: theorems/13_uncertainty_possible_sat.txt
Distinctive nonclassical possibility: p(A v ~A)<1.
Expected result: SAT.

Pr(A v ~A) < 1


EXAMPLE 14: DE MORGAN NEGATION NEED NOT BE COMPLEMENTARY
File: theorems/14_demorgan_noncomplementarity_sat.txt
Distinctive nonclassical possibility: p(~A) need not equal 1-p(A).
Expected result: SAT.

Pr(~A) != 1 - Pr(A)
